===Notable players===
{{see also|Category:Dragons RFC players}}

Michael Owen captained [[Wales national rugby union team|Wales]] in 2005–06 and he led Wales to their first [[Grand Slam (Rugby Union)|Grand Slam]] for 27-years in the [[2005 Six Nations Championship]].

[[Lewis Evans (rugby union)|Lewis Evans]] has made over 200 appearances for the Dragons. Prop [[Adam Black (rugby union)|Adam Black]] became the first centurion in Dragons colours during the 2006–07 season. The following players have also made over one hundred Dragons appearances: [[Jamie Ringer]], [[Peter Sidoli]], [[Gareth Wyatt]], [[Steve Jones (rugby union, born 1977)|Steve Jones]], [[Luke Charteris]], [[Wayne Evans (rugby union)|Wayne Evans]], [[Aled Brew]], [[Ashley Smith (rugby union)|Ashley Smith]], [[Adam Jones (rugby union, born 1980)|Adam Jones]], [[Hugh Gustafson]], [[Jason Tovey]], [[Robert Sidoli]], [[Phil Price (rugby union)|Phil Price]], [[Adam Hughes (rugby union, born 1990)|Adam Hughes]], [[Nic Cudd]], [[Ligtoring Landman|Rynard Landman]], [[Matthew Screech]], [[Brok Harris]], [[Lloyd Fairbrother]], [[Jack Dixon]], [[Elliot Dee]], [[Ashton Hewitt]], [[Joe Davies (rugby player)|Joe Davies]], [[Harrison Keddie]], [[Rhodri Williams (rugby player)|Rhodri Williams]], [[Ollie Griffiths]], [[Aaron Wainwright]].

Many former players were capped by the [[Wales national rugby union team]] while with the region; props [[Chris Anthony (rugby union)|Chris Anthony]], [[Rhys M. Thomas]] and [[Bradley Roberts (rugby union)|Bradley Roberts]], hookers [[Steve Jones (rugby union, born 1977)|Steve Jones]], [[Lloyd Burns]] and [[Leon Brown (rugby union)|Leon Brown]], second rows [[Ian Gough]], [[Luke Charteris]], [[Andrew Coombs]], [[Cory Hill]] and [[Will Rowlands]], flankers [[Jason Forster]], [[Richard Parks]], [[Jamie Ringer]], [[Gavin Thomas]] and [[Ross Moriarty]], number eight [[Michael Owen (rugby union)|Michael Owen]], [[Rhys Oakley]], [[Taulupe Faletau]], [[Dan Lydiate]], [[Ollie Griffiths]] and [[Taine Basham]], scrum halves [[Gareth Cooper]] and [[Andy Williams (rugby union)|Andy Williams]], outside half [[Ceri Sweeney]], centres [[Andy Marinos]], [[Tyler Morgan]] and [[Nick Tompkins]], wingers [[Gareth Wyatt]], [[Haldane Luscombe|Hal Luscombe]], [[Aled Brew]], [[Will Harries]], [[Tom Prydie]], [[Hallam Amos]], [[Jonah Holmes]] and fullback [[Kevin Morgan (rugby union)|Kevin Morgan]]. [[Percy Montgomery]], [[Sione Tuʻipulotu]], [[Rod Snow]], [[Mike Hercus]], [[Mike Petri]], [[James Arlidge]], [[Will Kelly (rugby union)|Will Kelly]], [[Mesake Doge]], [[Gonzalo Bertranou]] and [[Aki Seiuli]] played internationally for their respective countries whilst with the region.

Of the current players [[Elliot Dee]], [[Aaron Wainwright]], [[Ben Carter (rugby union)|Ben Carter]], [[Rio Dyer]], [[Matthew Screech]], [[Cai Evans]], [[Rhodri Williams (rugby union)|Rhodri Williams]], [[Christian Coleman]] and [[Brodie Coghlan]] have featured in Wales test matches whilst with the region. [[Rhodri Jones]], [[Shane Lewis-Hughes]], [[Seb Davies]], [[Dillon Lewis]], [[Wyn Jones (rugby union)|Wyn Jones]] and [[Thomas Young (rugby union)|Thomas Young]] achieved Wales international caps before joining the Dragons.
